About this prototype

Anna is a voice vocabulary tutor I built with Vapi for Apna’s application process. I used HTML to embed the live agent and hosted it on GitHub Pages.

She’s teach-first: she introduces one word, gives an example, asks you to use it in your own sentence, coaches your answer, and brings words you struggled with back two turns later.

I added strict scope and safety guardrails, so she declines off-topic and unsafe requests, and I tuned response speed after noticing long pauses.
